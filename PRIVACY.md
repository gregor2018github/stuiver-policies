# Privacy Policy — Stuiver

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Stuiver is a personal finance dashboard for private, single-user use. It is run locally by the
person whose financial data it processes. This policy describes what the application does with that
data.

## Who is responsible

Stuiver is operated by its individual user on their own equipment. There is no company, no hosted
service and no shared infrastructure. The person running the application is the controller of the
data it processes.

Contact: the email address registered with this application.

## What data is processed

- Account information retrieved from the user's own payment accounts: account identifiers, account
  holder name, currency and balances.
- Transaction data for those accounts: booking and value dates, amounts, currency, counterparty
  details where the bank provides them, and free-text payment descriptions.
- Financial data imported manually by the user, such as transaction exports downloaded from their
  bank.

No data about any person other than the user is collected deliberately. Counterparty names and
account numbers may appear within the user's own transaction records, as they would on any bank
statement.

## Where the data comes from

Account and transaction data is retrieved from the user's bank with the user's explicit consent,
through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account information service provider regulated by the Finnish
Financial Supervisory Authority. Access is granted by the user through their bank's own strong
customer authentication, is limited to the accounts the user selects, and expires after a period set
by the bank. The user can withdraw consent at any time, either in Stuiver or through their bank.

Enable Banking processes this data as part of providing that connection. Their handling of it is
governed by their own privacy policy at https://enablebanking.com/privacy/.

## Where the data is stored

All retrieved data is stored locally on the user's own machine, in a directory under the user's
control. Nothing is uploaded to a server operated by Stuiver, because no such server exists.

## What the data is used for

Solely to produce financial overviews for the user: categorised spending, balances over time, and
similar summaries displayed within the application.

The data is not sold, shared, published, or used for advertising, profiling, credit scoring, or
training machine learning models. It is not transmitted to any third party other than the bank and
Enable Banking connection described above.

## Analytics and tracking

None. The application performs no telemetry, contains no analytics, and sets no cookies for tracking
purposes.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained locally for as long as the user keeps it. The user can delete all stored data at
any time by removing the application's data directory. Revoking bank consent stops any further
retrieval.

## Rights

Because the user is both the controller and the sole data subject, rights of access, rectification,
erasure and portability are exercised directly: the data is on their own machine, in their own
files.

For data held by Enable Banking or by the user's bank, those organisations' own procedures apply.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The date at the top reflects the most recent
revision.
